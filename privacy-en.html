Privacy Policy

Atisba Scan app (com.jrsoft.escanerqr) · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This is a translation of the Spanish version. If the two differ, the Spanish version prevails.

In one sentence: Atisba Scan reads and creates codes inside your phone. There are no accounts, the camera image never leaves your phone and your history stays on it. The only thing that leaves it is technical data from the Google libraries that read the codes and serve the ads.

Who is responsible

The data controller is JR Soft, trading name of Javier Román Sáez, developer of the Atisba Scan app. You can get in touch at demual@gmail.com.

What this app does NOT do

  • There is no sign-up, account, username or password. You are never asked for an email address, a phone number or a name.
  • No analytics or usage-statistics tools are used: no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics and no crash reporting.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK (AdMob) collects device identifiers, as explained in this policy. Some US privacy laws may treat that as “selling” or “sharing” personal data (see “US residents”).
  • No image is uploaded. The camera image and the photos you choose are read inside your phone and are neither saved nor transmitted.
  • The content of the codes you read or create is not sent to any server. This app has no servers of its own.
  • Link checking (the traffic light) is done inside your phone, without looking anything up online. The app does not visit links on its own: they only open when you ask (or if you turn on “Open green links right away” in Settings).
  • It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendar or files. When you save a contact, an event, a Wi-Fi network or a file, you do it through Android's own screens, which ask you to confirm.

Permissions

  • Camera, to scan. It is requested the first time you go to scan, with an explanation first. Without it, the app can still read codes from photos and create codes.
  • Internet and advertising identifier (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), which the advertising needs (see below).

Data that stays only on your phone

This information is kept in the app's private storage. It disappears if you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings.

  • Your history: the content of the codes you scan and create, with their date and format and, if you add them, a name and the favorite star. You can delete it in the app itself or stop saving it (Settings → Save history).
  • Two-step verification keys (otpauth:// codes) are never saved in the history.
  • “My card”, if you create it: the contact details you type in.
  • Your settings: language, theme, sound, vibration, search engine and the other options.
  • A photo you share with Atisba Scan from another app is copied to the app's temporary storage so it can be read.

Android backup. If you have system backup turned on, Android may include this data in your Google backup (managed by Google, not by this app), so it can be restored when you switch phones. You can turn it off in Android settings.

What you choose to share

  • Exporting, importing, saving and sharing. Files (PNG, PDF, CSV or TXT) are saved, opened or shared through Android's own screens, and only the file you choose.
  • Clipboard. The app reads the clipboard only when you tap “Create from copied text” or “Paste a map link”, and copies to it when you ask (or when scanning, if you turn on automatic copying).
  • Opening a link. It opens in a tab of your browser. The website receives your visit like any other, under its own terms.
  • Searching for a product or a text. What you search for is sent to the search engine you choose in Settings (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Yandex or Baidu).
  • Calling, writing, opening a map, saving a contact or an event, connecting to a Wi-Fi network. The system apps open with the details already filled in, and you are the one who confirms.

Data that does leave your phone

Code reading: Google ML Kit

The scanner works inside your phone with ML Kit, a Google library included in the app. Neither the images nor the content of the codes leave your phone. What ML Kit does send to Google is technical data about how it is working: the phone's make, model and system version, the app version, a random installation identifier (which, according to Google, is not intended to identify you or your phone), response times and error codes. Google uses this data to diagnose and improve that library, and states that it does not share it with third parties. The app does not use ML Kit's automatic zoom, which would also send data about each zoom session.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app is free and is supported by unobtrusive Google AdMob ads, which only appear in the history and in the generator's list of types: never on the camera screen, on a scan result or in full screen. Ad requests carry no data about what you scan or what you create.

As with any app that uses AdMob, Google's SDK collects the Android advertising identifier (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads), the app set identifier (app set ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), your interactions with the app and with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to serve and measure advertising, for statistics and to prevent fraud, under its own policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before serving personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you will see non-personalized advertising and the app works exactly the same.

Ratings and Google Play

Every now and then, after you have used it successfully, the app may ask you for a rating through Google Play's official window, which is managed by Google. “Rate the app” and “More apps by JR Soft” open Google Play.

IP address

As with any app that connects to the internet, the Google services mentioned above receive your IP address when connecting. This app has no servers of its own, so it keeps no logs of any kind.

Legal basis and retention

Processing relies on your consent, which you give when you grant the camera permission and, for personalized advertising, through Google's form, and which you can withdraw at any time from Android settings or from the app itself.

Data stored on your phone remains there until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data. This app keeps no data outside your phone; Google keeps its own data according to its policies.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union, the GDPR grants you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. In practice, and given that this app keeps none of your data outside your phone:

  • Access and portability: export your history as CSV or TXT from the app itself.
  • Erasure: delete your history from the app, or uninstall it. There is no need to ask anyone.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent: revoke the camera permission in Android settings or change your advertising choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

You can also write to the contact address or lodge a complaint with your country's supervisory authority. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

US residents

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to ask that your personal data not be sold or shared for targeted advertising. You can do this in the app: Settings → “Ad privacy options” → “Don't sell or share my data”. From then on, you will only see non-personalised ads. The app does not collect sensitive data; for ads, Google's SDK uses device identifiers and an approximate location (at city level). For any other request about your data, write to us at demual@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
